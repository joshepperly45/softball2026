ASA lineup rules

  • Use exactly 10 or 12 players.
  • Girls must hit 3rd, 6th, and 9th. A 12-player lineup also requires a girl hitting 12th.
  • Defense must include at least one female infielder and one female outfielder.
  • In a 12-player lineup, one male and one female must be marked DH.
